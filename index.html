What you get

Till, kitchen, owner phone, and a plain-language day.

One product. The kitchen display is optional. You do not buy four separate tools.

  1. 01

    Till

    On the phone or iPad you already own. Menu, cart, send the order to the kitchen. Runs in the browser — not a box under the counter.

  2. 02

    Kitchen display

    Optional. Tickets the kitchen can work from without shouting across the counter. Skip it if a two-person shop still calls orders out loud.

  3. 03

    Owner reports

    On your phone: sales, stock, wastage, cash close. The day’s numbers, not a spreadsheet rebuilt at midnight.

  4. 04

    Ask how the day went

    Plain answers from real till numbers. Ask how the day went — sales, stock, wastage, cash close — without learning a report screen.

What you don’t buy

No card machine. No required printer. No Mac mini.

The short list of things other tills make you pay for that Kade simply does not need.

  • Not payment processing. Cash or card is a tag on the order. We do not take the payment.
  • Not a printer you have to buy. Today receipts print or save as a PDF. Sending them on WhatsApp is next — that is coming, not here yet.
  • Not a Mac mini or special hardware. No server in the back room, no branded till. The browser, on the device you have.

  • Monthly service

  • Quoted, not listed
